Overview

3D Brick Breaker is a single-player arcade game. Gameplay, settings, and progress stay on your device, and the app does not require an account. A banner at the bottom of the screen may load advertisements from Google when a network connection is available. This policy explains what stays on your device, what the ad banner uses, and how you can remove local data.

Information stored on your device

The game saves the following in local app storage so your progress is still there the next time you open it:

  • Settings such as audio, graphics, controls, and accessibility options
  • Campaign progress, unlocked worlds, stars, and level records
  • Scores, best scores, coins, XP, profile rank, and achievements
  • Cosmetic unlocks and the paddle, ball, trail, impact, and arena looks you have equipped
  • Daily challenge completion for the local calendar day

This information is created by the game itself and stays in local storage. The game does not attach it to your name, email address, or phone number.

Android backup

The app allows Android backup. If you use your device’s backup feature, Android may include this app’s local data in that backup. That backup is managed by your device and your account settings, not by this game.

Save backup and restore

Settings includes a Backup / Restore Save action. It creates a text copy of your progress that stays on this device unless you copy or share that text yourself. Restoring a backup replaces the progress stored in the app.

Accounts and network

The app does not create accounts and does not include a separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK. It uses the internet to request banner ads and a full-screen ad when a game ends, through Google Mobile Ads. Gameplay itself does not need a connection.

Advertising

A banner at the bottom of the screen shows ads from Google AdMob. About every 45 seconds, the banner loads a different ad unit. When a game ends, a full-screen ad may appear. You close it with the close control on that ad. Google may process an advertising identifier, IP address, device information, and ad interactions to deliver and measure those ads, under Google’s own policies. You can limit ad personalization in your device settings. This game does not sell your progress or send it to the ad network.

How to delete your data

  • Reset All Progress in Settings clears campaign unlocks, stars, coins, XP, achievements, cosmetics, and personal best scores on this device. Settings stay intact.
  • Reset Settings restores audio, graphics, control, and accessibility options. Progress stays intact.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its local storage from the device.

There is no remote copy for the developer to delete.

Children

3D Brick Breaker is a general-audience arcade game. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will appear in the app with a new effective date.
